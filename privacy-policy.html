Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Short version: DevBridge works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Ever.

1. Who we are

DevBridge is a Chrome browser extension for frontend developers and QA engineers. It is developed and maintained as an independent project. For questions, contact: devbridge.ext@gmail.com

2. What data DevBridge accesses

DevBridge reads network request and response data from the browser tab you are actively inspecting inside Chrome DevTools. This includes:

  • Request URLs, HTTP methods, headers, and body
  • Response status codes, headers, and body
  • CSS computed styles of elements you click with the Design Token picker

3. How your data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device only using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This means:

  • Data never leaves your computer
  • Data is never sent to any external server
  • Data is never shared with third parties
  • Data is automatically cleared when you uninstall the extension

4. What data is NOT collected

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, address)
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not use analytics or tracking pixels
  • We do not have user accounts or authentication
  • We do not monetize your data in any way

5. Permissions explained

DevBridge requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • tabs — Required to identify the currently active tab so the extension can relay clipboard operations via the content script.
  • storage — Required to persist intercept rules, mock snapshots, and settings locally on your device between sessions.
  • declarativeNetRequest — Required for the Intercept feature, which lets you redirect matching network requests to a custom mock response you define.
  • clipboardWrite — Required to copy generated mock code to your clipboard when you click the Copy button.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — Required so the DevTools network panel can capture requests from any website you are inspecting. No data from these requests is sent anywhere.

6. Third-party services

DevBridge does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data services. The extension makes no outbound network requests of its own.

7. Children's privacy

DevBridge is a developer tool intended for use by adults. It does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
